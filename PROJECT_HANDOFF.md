## Stack and architecture

The package has no component library, database, authentication, payment processing or live signup integration. next.config.js permits image qualities 75 and 90; tsconfig.json maps @/* to src/*. Review relevant node_modules/next/dist/docs/ guidance before changing Next.js code, per AGENTS.md.

## Approved visual system and Home

Use the palette tokens in src/app/globals.css: blue #206B8D, near-black #1C1C1C, body #383838, white #FFFFFF, Welcome tint #E8F6FA, Visit warm #F7F2EB and newsletter neutral #F4F4F4. Legacy cyan #00A1CA stays defined but is not used for new UI. Derived translucent colors use RGB token channels. Georgia and Arial/Helvetica are the site faces; only the footer uses its scoped Lora and Literata via next/font/google. Do not introduce another visual style. JRK was a layout/measurement reference, not a source of branding, assets, copy or code.

Home order is **Welcome → beach shore → Menu/Catering/Our Story cards → newsletter**. The full-width white desktop header has left navigation, a centered PEARL ISLAND wordmark and right social/Order Now actions. At widths below 1100px it becomes a phone header with a full-screen menu, focus trap, Escape, focus return, scroll lock and safe-area support. The same header fades/lifts through the first 140px of scrolling; farther down it hides, then returns fixed after at least 8px upward scroll with a 320ms slide/fade and shadow. Its place in the document is reserved so content does not jump. Keyboard focus and an open phone menu keep it visible; reduced motion is immediate.

Welcome is a full-width #E8F6FA band. At 900px and wider it has two vertically centered columns and a minimum height of the viewport below the header; on smaller screens the heading, description, Order Now and photo stack in that order. The current kitchen photo is welcome-kitchen.jpg, rendered black and white with object-position 42% 55%. The beach hero is a still photo, not video, between Welcome and cards; it has a solid Welcome-colored top wave, white bottom wave, subtle edge fades, and bounded viewport-relative photo drift without exposed gaps. Its current height and crop are deliberate. Home reveals and photo zoom replay when elements reenter; clicking Home/wordmark while already home jumps to top and replays the introduction. Without JavaScript content remains visible; reduced motion disables the effects.

The cards retain a 3:4 shape, 22px radius, gloss, fade and giant white bottom-left Georgia titles. Menu links to /menu; Catering and Our Story are inactive. On desktop fine pointers they tilt up to 4.5 degrees with 2px lift and subtle photo counter-drift; touch and reduced-motion are static. The newsletter is #F4F4F4; its email and Sign Up controls are natively disabled and explain their inactive state to assistive technology. No signup result or external service exists. The dark footer remains static: Home/Menu/Catering/Our Story links, a smaller outlined Order Now, Location, Contact, social links, Gift Cards and Apply Here. The footer lists no Community, Press, TikTok or hours.

## Menu design and behavior

Only the **Tropical Poster** design remains. Design A (Menu Magazine), its review switch and ?design=a|b behavior were removed; /menu ignores that query. The blue hero has white Georgia headline “Island Flavor.”, a short eyebrow/subtitle and decorative translucent rings. A sleek white category bar sits immediately below it. Its eight tabs, in order, are Platters, Sides, Family Style, Salads, Drinks, Pearl Island Products, Wing Wednesdays and Oxtail. Tabs replace content without anchor jumps; the active blue underline slides, the phone row scrolls horizontally, and arrow-key navigation/focus styling are supported. Category headings share one position and scale.

Menu images are transparent PNG cutouts in public/images/menu/ with natural dimensions and shape classes in menu-data.ts: PLATTER, SIDE_TRAY, CUP, TALL and WIDE_TRAY. Next Image uses quality 90, contain sizing, no cropping or upscaling, transparent stages, a shared soft image drop shadow and an extra contact shadow for tall containers. There are no white photo boxes, plate halos or blend-mode tricks. Two menu items without photos use a PI monogram. The images are generated **demo** media, not approved product photography. Nine protein cutouts and other menu imagery must be replaced with real Pearl Island photos before launch; wings-curry is the current Vegan Wings stand-in.

The item grids use the **Classic Editorial** layout: whole-item buttons, no card border/background/shadow, image stage above a left-aligned Georgia name and a separate muted Arial surcharge/price line. The image-name gap is 18px; desktop/phone row gaps are 48px/36px. Existing fine-pointer hover gently lifts/scales the image and darkens the name; do not add an underline. Category items fade in with a 6px rise over 420ms and 35ms stagger (maximum 280ms), while the container only fades; reduced motion removes these effects.

Clicking a menu item opens the reusable native-dialog **minimal editorial split modal**: large contained image on the left, name, price, supplied description and a full-width solid Order Now on the right; mobile stacks them. The top-right × button has accessible name “Close”. Escape/backdrop close it, focus remains trapped while open and returns to the originating item, and background scrolling locks. There is **no combo line in the modal**. Ordering uses Square, with no cart, selection state, checkout or price calculation on this site.

Platter proteins, in fixed order, are Jerk Chicken (no extra charge), Roasted Pork (+$1.25), Curry Chicken (none), Sous Poulet (none), Curry Tofu (+$0.25), Jerk Tofu (+$0.25), Creole Beans (+$0.25), Sous Shu (+$1.25) and Chickpea Stew (+$1.50). The tinted **Platter Options** section has four equal-height cards: Half Platter $12.00 (one protein, two-thirds portion), Platter $14.75 (one protein, regular portion), Half & Half Platter $19.50 (two proteins), and Trifecta Platter $24.25 (three proteins). The cards retain “Make it a combo / Includes a drink / from +$2”; final selections and totals belong in Square. Desktop shows four across, phone two by two.

Sides and drinks currently display surcharges relative to a stated $2.00 base; this interpretation still needs Sober's confirmation. Pearl Island Products has six items; PI Signature Drinks was intentionally removed. Wing Wednesdays is **Food Gallery First**, display-only: intro, Chicken/Vegan wing photos, two nonclickable serving-option cards (Wing Platter $18.00, six pieces; Wing Basket $16.00, eight pieces), sauce line and an ORDER ONLINE Square link. No old four-option cards, “New” pill or prep-time/add-ons line. Every category ends in an aligned ordering row. Family Style and Oxtail have informational cards, not add-to-cart controls.

The separate **Visit & pickup** section sits after category content and before the unchanged site footer on every Menu tab. It uses warm #F7F2EB, information first and a Google Maps iframe second (stacked on phone), for 221 Carlton Road, Suite 1, Charlottesville, VA 22902, inside B.E.A.C.O.N Shared Kitchen.
